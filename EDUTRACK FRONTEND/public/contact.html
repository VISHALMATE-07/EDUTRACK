Frequently Asked Questions
Is EduTrack free to use? ▾
Yes — this is currently a prototype, and all demo accounts are free to explore.
Is my data real or stored anywhere? ▾
No. This version of EduTrack is a frontend-only prototype using demo data. Nothing you enter is saved to a real server yet.
How do I switch between student and faculty view? ▾
Each role has its own login. Log out and choose the other role from the "Choose Login Type" screen.
Can I use EduTrack on my phone? ▾
Yes, the entire interface is responsive and designed to work well on mobile, tablet and desktop.
