About Us

A clearer way to see your academic and career progress.

EduTrack was built around a simple idea: students and faculty shouldn't need five different tools — a portal for marks, a spreadsheet for attendance, a group chat for announcements, a notebook for career planning — just to understand how things are going.

Our Mission

Give every student a clear picture of their academic standing and career readiness, and give every faculty member the visibility to support their class effectively.

Academic + Career, Together

Grades and attendance tell only half the story. EduTrack connects academic performance with career skill-building, so progress in one area supports the other.

Student & Faculty Collaboration

Classrooms keep announcements, material and assignments in sync between students and faculty, instead of scattered across channels.

Toward Intelligent Guidance

This prototype includes a preview of an AI Advisor experience — the groundwork for guidance that's grounded in each student's real data, once connected to a live AI API.

Currently a frontend prototype

EduTrack is currently a frontend-only demo built with HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. It uses realistic demo data throughout. The architecture is designed to connect cleanly to a Node.js + Express backend, Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Firebase Storage as the project grows.
