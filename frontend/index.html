Why EduTrack?

Everything that shapes your academic standing and career readiness, organized so it's easy to act on — not just look at.

Academic Tracking

Subjects, marks, attendance and assignments in one connected view, with clear warnings when something needs attention.

Career Development

Set a career goal, track skill progress, and see exactly what to learn next to close the gap.

Classroom Communication

Announcements, study material and assignments from every classroom, without digging through chat threads.

Internship Readiness

See how you match up against internship opportunities, and what skills would raise your match score.

Keep your resume, certificates and mark sheets organized in one private vault.
